About — the short version

For seventeen years I've worked where code meets people — writing the first line, hiring the tenth engineer, building teams that look like the world they serve.

I began as an engineer and learned to build the teams that build everything else. I founded my first company at 26, shipped thirty-odd apps, and led engineering across mobility, climate, IoT and AI. The throughline never moved: make things that matter, with people who care.

Today I lead engineering at scale, run a small studio after hours, and build for causes I believe in. I write code and poetry with the same hands — and I'm certain the best technology, like the best teams, is built to serve people first.
